# Career gallery

Thirty fictional career portraits generated individually for the SponsorReach landing page. They illustrate professions, not customers, testimonials or people who received sponsorship through the product. They are presented as occupation illustrations without any customer identity or testimonial claims.

Occupation choices were checked against the application's occupation taxonomy in sm.concepts. Display labels shorten some taxonomy names for readability, including Resident doctors, Software developer, Laboratory technicians, Vehicle technicians/mechanics/electricians, Plumbers and heating/ventilating installers, Electricians and electrical fitters, Waiters and waitresses, Warehouse operatives, and Receptionists. A role's presence does not establish a current sponsored vacancy or individual eligibility.

Two rows display fifteen careers each and move in opposite directions. Duplicate visual tracks make the loop continuous while keeping duplicate cards out of the accessibility tree. Pointer hover and keyboard focus do not pause the rows. The gallery has no pause button. Reduced-motion settings replace animation with manually scrollable rows.

No fabricated reviews, testimonials or user statistics are included.

## Delivery checks

Thirty independently generated images were visually inspected together in a browser grid. WebP delivery copies retain the original image dimensions and total about 3.3 MB, compared with 68.5 MB of original PNGs. Originals are retained in brand/career-portraits-originals and the image generation directory.

The gallery contains no pause/resume control, hover pausing or visible AI/disclaimer caption, following the user's final revisions. Reduced-motion preferences produce manually scrollable static rows. The gallery makes no claim that these people are customers. No invented user counter or testimonials were added.

Local production build and desktop/mobile layout checks passed. Both rows run on hover with opposing animation directions. All 30 assets loaded in the local visual inspection, and all 30 staged assets returned HTTP 200.

## Published verification
