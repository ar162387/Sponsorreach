## Purchase and delivery

SponsorReach Monthly costs USD $19.99 per month, plus any applicable tax shown before payment. It renews automatically unless cancelled. AgentaOS is the merchant of record and handles payment and receipts; customers enter card information on its hosted checkout. A confirmed payment provides 800 research credits for that paid billing period. Do not describe the settlement token returned by the product API as the customer's fiat charge currency.

## Using credits

The first paid batch contains up to 20 companies and costs one credit, including companies already shown in the preview. A revised search costs one credit when its first nonempty results batch is durably delivered. Each additional previously unopened nonempty batch of up to 20 companies costs one credit. Cost is shown before the action. Refreshing, reopening unlocked results, retries, failures and empty results cost nothing. Credits reset at the end of the paid period and do not roll over; renewing supplies 800 for the new period. This is not 800 searches or a guarantee of results.

## Cancellation and saved research

Cancel renewal through the account before the next payment. Cancellation is scheduled for the end of the paid period; it does not automatically refund a payment. Remaining credits remain usable until that period ends. No new allowance is issued after a failed renewal. Already unlocked account research remains available after cancellation, subject to account deletion, correction or legitimate withdrawal of information. Do not publish this persistence promise before expiry queries and purge jobs are updated.

## Refunds

Request a full voluntary refund within 14 days of the first subscription payment, including after some credit use. This voluntary promise does not restart on renewals. Statutory remedies for faulty, missing or misdescribed services remain unaffected. The refund request flow also clearly asks to stop future renewal. A pending request is not a completed refund: AgentaOS reviews and processes the request. Confirmed refunds remove only the remaining allowance from the affected paid period; no debt is created and unlocked research remains accessible. Provide support escalation if the provider declines a request covered by the published promise.

## Research limitations and acceptable use

Customers receive research functionality and organised information for their own employer research. A licence does not establish an open vacancy, willingness to sponsor a particular role, an introduction or an offer. No reply, employment or sponsorship outcome is guaranteed. Prohibit bulk redistribution, impersonation, harassment and unsolicited mass campaigns. Preserve mandatory consumer rights and the service's obligation to exercise reasonable care and skill.
