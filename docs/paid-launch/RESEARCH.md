## Launch implications

- [Checkout.com disclosures](https://support.checkout.com/hc/en-us/articles/15455392982802-Verify-your-website) cover identity, jurisdiction, support and policies. They are useful benchmarks, not assurance of approval elsewhere.
- [Stripe restrictions](https://stripe.com/legal/restricted-businesses) prohibit misleading business information and undisclosed processing. Applications and the real product must agree.
- [Government work-finding guidance](https://www.gov.uk/employment-agencies-and-businesses) says websites mainly providing work-finding may fall within employment-agency rules, including restrictions on charging jobseekers. Obtain an assessment of this actual matching/credits offer; a research-software label alone does not settle it.
- [IAA guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/iaa-adviser-registration-explained/iaa-adviser-registration-explained) identifies individual immigration advice as an activity requiring appropriate regulatory status. Keep the product focused on company research.
- [ICO guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/) explains that identifiable business contacts can remain personal information when public. Assess collection, display, notice and objections. Do not assume every individual employment enquiry is direct marketing; purpose and circumstances matter.
- [Online-selling rules](https://www.gov.uk/online-and-distance-selling-for-businesses/online-selling) require appropriate pre-contract information and distinguish digital-content cancellation steps. Determine service/content classification before relying on exceptions; preserve [mandatory consumer rights](https://www.gov.uk/unfair-terms-in-sales-contracts/implied-rights).

## Recommendation

Sell personalised employer research: relevant companies, reasons and available routes. Do not imply employment placement, exclusive access to a public list, employer endorsement or guaranteed outcomes. Named employee contacts remain part of the actual disclosure when available; do not claim no information is supplied.

A future underwriting enquiry should include the actual operator, walkthrough, credit charges, source/rights summary and support/refund arrangements. Ask which category or requirement prevents support. No enquiry has been sent; do not reapply simply because copy has changed.
