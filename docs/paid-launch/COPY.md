### FAQs

**What am I getting?** A research tool for identifying relevant UK licensed sponsor companies. Results bring together company context, reasons for relevance and available routes for your own research and outreach.

**How is this different from the public sponsor register?** The register identifies licensed organisations. SponsorReach adds professional relevance, supporting company context and available routes, organised around the background you provide.

**Does a licence mean a company will sponsor me?** No. A licence does not establish a current vacancy, willingness to sponsor your role or an offer. Check each opportunity with the employer.

**Will every company include an employee contact?** No. We show available company routes and include relevant employee contacts only when supported by the information available. We do not promise a named contact or a reply.

**What uses a credit?** A search with results uses 1 credit for its first batch of up to 20 companies. Loading each additional batch uses 1 credit. A revised search follows the same rule. We show the cost before you continue. Reopening unlocked results, failed searches and searches without results are free.

**What happens after my free preview?** You can buy credits to explore more results. Opening the first paid batch costs 1 credit and includes the companies already shown in your preview; it is not 20 additional companies. You choose whether to load more.

**Do credits expire?** There is no scheduled expiry. Credits remain attached to your account while it and the service operate. The Terms explain account closure and what happens if we discontinue paid access.

**Can I revisit saved results?** Yes, without spending another credit. Details can change, and information may be corrected or removed. Check current information before approaching a company.

**Can I get a refund?** Request a full refund within 14 days of purchase, even if you have used some credits. You also retain statutory rights if something is faulty, missing or not as described. See our refund policy for contact details.
